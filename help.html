How to Use the Blueprint Tracker

Plan your crafting in Subnautica 2. Queue blueprints, track your ingredients, and mark items as crafted as you go.

⬡
Blueprint Browser
Left panel — browse and select blueprints
⬡
Browse by Station & Category
Use the station tabs to switch crafting stations (Fabricator, Mobile Bay, etc.). Use the category pills to narrow by type. Press Esc to clear search.
◈
Select & Add to Queue
Click once to preview the blueprint in the center panel. Click again (or use Add to Queue) to add it. If it's already queued, the quantity increases.

Blueprint Detail
Center panel — preview ingredients and quantities
◎
Ingredient Overview
Each ingredient tile shows an icon with have / need below it. A green border means your inventory already covers that ingredient. Click a craftable ingredient to jump to its blueprint.
+
Set Quantity & Add
Adjust the quantity stepper then click Add to Queue. Have/need counts reflect your live inventory and update instantly whenever it changes.
→
Multi-Output Yields
Some recipes produce more than one item per craft — e.g. Distraction Flare × 3 or Salvaged Titanium → 4× Titanium. A teal Yields N× Name banner appears below the ingredients when this applies. The quantity you set is the number of crafts, not output items.

Typical Workflow
From planning to crafting
1
Find a blueprint
Browse or search for what you want to build. Click its icon to preview it in the center panel.
2
Add to the queue
Set the quantity and click Add to Queue, or click the icon again to add the current quantity instantly.
3
Queue sub-components if needed
Check the Total Resources Required card. If a craftable material is missing, click its + button to queue it for exactly the shortfall amount.
4
Log what you gather
As you collect materials in-game, use the ingredient steppers (or the Have column in the summary) to record your stock.
5
Mark as Crafted
Once Mark as Crafted unlocks (all ingredients satisfied), click it. Resources are consumed and the crafted item is added to inventory automatically.
6
Batch craft when ready
Use Craft All (N) in the header to complete everything ready in one click.
◈
Tips
◈ Your data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage — no account or internet connection needed. It persists across sessions as long as you use the same browser.
◈ Ingredient quantities are shared across all queued blueprints. If two items both need Titanium, the steppers and summary reflect the combined total against the same inventory pool.
◈ Click any ingredient in the detail panel to jump directly to that ingredient's own blueprint — useful for planning deep crafting chains.
◈ Collapse All in the queue header folds all cards so you can see the full list without scrolling. Use Expand All to reopen them.
◈ Clear Crafted removes all completed items from the queue, keeping only blueprints whose ingredients haven't been fully gathered yet.
◈ Press Esc in the search bar to instantly clear the search and return to the current station view.
