How to Use the SN2 Blueprint Tracker

  1. Browse blueprints — Use the station tabs (Fabricator, Processor, Habitat Builder, etc.) and category filters to find any blueprint. Search by name using the search bar at the top of the blueprint panel.
  2. Add to your build queue — Click any blueprint to open its detail view, set the quantity you want to build, and click Add to Queue.
  3. Check your ingredient totals — The Build Queue panel automatically totals every raw material you need across all queued blueprints, so you know exactly what to gather.
  4. Mark items as crafted — Once you've built something in-game, mark it as crafted. Output materials are added to your inventory and automatically applied toward other recipes in your queue.
  5. Track your inventory — Switch to the Inventory tab to see everything you've collected, so you know what you still need to find.

Tips

  • Your build queue, inventory, and crafted items are saved in your browser — no account needed, and everything persists between sessions.
  • Multi-output recipes (like Salvaged Titanium → 4× Titanium) are handled automatically when you mark them crafted.
